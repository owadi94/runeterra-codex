About

What the Codex is

Runeterra Codex is a fan project for people who love the stories behind League of Legends. It turns the lore into short games, so a new player can learn the world in an evening and a veteran can test what they know.

Where the lore comes from

Every summary is written in our own words. Facts are checked against Riot Games' official Universe site. When sources disagree, we follow the newest official version. If you spot a mistake, tell us and we'll fix it.

Your data

There are no accounts and no tracking. Your scores, streak and progress are saved only in this browser on this device.
