About

About the Codex

Runeterra Codex is a fan project for people who love League of Legends. It turns champions and lore into short games, so a new player can learn the world in an evening and a veteran can test what they know.

Where the content comes from

Champion names, titles, abilities and art come live from Riot's Data Dragon service, so they stay up to date with each patch. Lore summaries are written in our own words and checked against Riot's official Universe site.

Your data

There are no accounts and no tracking. Your scores, streak and progress are saved only in this browser.
